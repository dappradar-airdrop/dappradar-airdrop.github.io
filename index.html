What is DappRadar Airdrop?

DappRadar is a widely used dapp analytics platform — sometimes described as an "app store for dapps" — that tracks decentralized applications, tokens, NFTs and on-chain activity across many blockchains. Its airdrop tracker curates lists of ongoing and potential token airdrops so you can discover and compare them in one place.

"DappRadar Airdrop" on this page means using that research tool to find and evaluate airdrops. This is an independent, informational reference and is not affiliated with or endorsed by DappRadar. It does not distribute tokens and has no claim flow — actual participation always happens directly with the project, on its own verified site.

What is the RADAR token?

RADAR is the utility and governance token associated with the DappRadar ecosystem. It is an ERC-20 token used for governance and rewards within DappRadar's products. Holding RADAR is not required to read airdrop information, and this page does not sell, give away or "claim" RADAR.

As with any token, the same ticker can be copied by imposters and can appear on multiple networks. Always confirm the official RADAR contract address from DappRadar's own documentation before interacting with any token that claims to be RADAR.

How airdrop tracking works

DappRadar aggregates activity across many chains and curates airdrop listings with a short description, the tasks or actions a project mentions, and eligibility notes. You use it as a research and discovery tool: browse candidates, read what each project actually requires, and open the project's own official channels to verify details.

Nothing on this informational page performs an airdrop, checks eligibility, or lets you claim. Any real interaction — signing up, completing tasks, or claiming — happens on the project's verified website, never here, and never by connecting a wallet to this page.

How to evaluate an airdrop

Before spending time or money on any airdrop, sanity-check it:

  • Verify the source: find the project's official domain and social accounts, and cross-check with independent analytics like DappRadar.
  • Read the requirements: legitimate airdrops reward real usage; be wary of tasks that push you to sign approvals or send funds.
  • Check the token contract: confirm the exact address rather than trusting a ticker or logo.
  • Beware urgency: countdowns and "limited spots" are classic pressure tactics.
  • Assume nothing is guaranteed: potential airdrops may never happen, and value is never assured. Not financial advice.

Spotting airdrop scams

Airdrop scams imitate real campaigns to steal funds. The warning signs are consistent:

  • Seed-phrase requests: anyone asking for your recovery phrase or private keys is a scammer, always.
  • Upfront payment: a "gas fee", "unlock fee" or "verification deposit" to claim is a scam.
  • Fake claim pages: a wallet prompt to approve or transfer tokens can drain your wallet — read every signature.
  • Lookalike domains: imposters copy names and logos on near-identical URLs.
  • Unsolicited "you won" messages: DMs, comments and emails promising a surprise airdrop are usually bait.

See the FTC's guidance on recognising and avoiding phishing scams.

Staying safe

Treat airdrop hunting as high-risk research. Read the notices below before you act on any listing.

Confirm the project's official domain, verify the token contract from DappRadar's documentation, and read every wallet signature request. Never approve a token allowance or transfer to claim an airdrop from an unverified site.

Networks & where RADAR lives

DappRadar's analytics span many layer-2 and layer-1 networks, so airdrop listings can involve tokens on Ethereum, its rollups and other chains. Each opportunity has its own network and requirements, which the project defines.

The RADAR token itself is an ERC-20 on Ethereum, with bridged versions on some networks such as Polygon and BNB Chain. Because bridged and imitation tokens can share the RADAR name, always confirm the official contract on the network you are using before interacting.
